# DGM (formerly DOGERMANCOIN) — Copyright & Attribution Notice

**DGM** (previously known as **DOGERMANCOIN**) is an open-source, custom-built cryptocurrency project. While the network has been rebranded to **DGM**, the underlying architecture and core mechanics remain rooted in the original **DOGERMANCOIN** design. 

This project is an independently written codebase developed from scratch and is not a fork of Bitcoin, Litecoin, or any other existing blockchain. We respect and acknowledge the foundational principles established by the pioneers of the decentralized ecosystem.

---

## Conceptual Inspiration

While the codebase is entirely original and custom-coded, the conceptual framework of DGM draws inspiration from:

- **The Bitcoin Whitepaper**  
  The revolutionary concept of decentralized peer-to-peer electronic cash introduced by *Satoshi Nakamoto*.

- **The Global Open-Source Blockchain Community**  
  The collaborative efforts of developers, researchers, and cryptography enthusiasts who have shaped the blockchain space through platforms like [GitHub](https://github.com).
